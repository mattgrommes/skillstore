# Site Instructions Skill

## Booking an appointment

1. Click on 'Book Appointment' in the top toolbar
2. Click on the type of class you want to schedule in the Class Type list
3. Use the calendar to click on the date you want to schedule
4. If there are two classes on the same day, choose the instructor you want
5 Confirm the class details in the Confirm box
6. Click schedule

## Signing up for a membership

1. Click on 'New Membership!'
2. Fill out the personal information form and hit 'Next'
3. Choose your class types (yoga, spin, etc.)
